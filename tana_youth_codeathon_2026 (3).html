Frequently Asked Questions

TANA CodeAthon is an online coding competition designed to inspire innovation and technical excellence in middle and high school students.

  • Challenges students to build a functional video game from scratch based on a provided prompt.
  • Provides a platform to demonstrate unique coding logic, creative design, and storytelling abilities.

Eligibility is determined by the student's age as of August 1, 2026 across two distinct divisions:

Middle School Division
Under 14 years old
High School Division
Ages 14 to 18 years old

This is a 100% Individual & Online competition. Each participant develops their own unique vision and showcases their personal coding skills without team dependencies.

Freedom of Choice: Students are encouraged to use whatever engines they are most comfortable with! Suggested choices include:

Godot Scratch Unity Python / PyGame HTML5 / JS

Important Requirement: All games must be designed to run directly inside a standard web browser (must support web export).

Submissions must be emailed to tanacodeathon2026@gmail.com containing:

  1. Participant ID & Title: Assigned Participant ID, Division, and official Game Title.
  2. Public Game URL: Web-hosted game link (Itch.io recommended, Scratch allowed, download-free).
  3. 1-2 Min Demo Video Pitch: Video showcasing gameplay, visual design, audio, and unique mechanics.
Code of Conduct & Guidelines

Competition Rules

70% Core Originality

At least 70% of the game logic, code, art, and core content must be the student's original work created specifically for TANA CodeAthon 2026.

External & AI Attribution

Third-party royalty-free audio, open-source sprites, or AI-assisted content are permitted, provided they are explicitly credited in the submission email & game credits.

ESRB E10+ Rating Standard

Family Friendly Content Policy

All submitted games must strictly adhere to an ESRB rating of E10+ or lower. Games containing inappropriate language, excessive violence, or offensive themes will be immediately disqualified.

Evaluation Criteria

Judging Rubric

Creativity & Design

Originality of game concept, visual aesthetics, narrative depth, and thematic innovation.

Code Quality

Logic structure, bug-free execution, clean mechanics implementation, and performance stability.

User Experience

Intuitive controls, game feel, engaging level progression, sound effects, and overall replayability.
